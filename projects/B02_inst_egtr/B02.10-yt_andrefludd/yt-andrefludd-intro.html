📺 YouTube · Andre Fludd

One page per video from Andre Fludd's channel. Each page takes a single video, pulls out what is actually being claimed, and works through it slowly enough to be usable at the instrument.

Why a separate section. Guitar videos are dense and badly indexed: ten minutes of playing with three real ideas buried in it, and no way to search what you half-remember six weeks later. The rest of this section is a system I'm building; this folder is other people's ideas, kept separate so the two don't get confused.

The format of each page, in this order:

  1. the idea in one sentence,
  2. a numbered summary of every idea in the video,
  3. a terminology table — the jargon, decoded,
  4. a deep dive that follows the video's own chapters,
  5. what to pair it with next, and how it connects to the rest of these notes.

One honest limitation. These pages are built from the video's transcript plus what can be derived from theory. Anything that only exists on screen — the exact tab, the fingering, the fret positions — is not reproduced here. Where a page says watch the screen for this, it means it: the notes are the analysis, the video is the source.
