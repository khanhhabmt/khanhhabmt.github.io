🎛️ Electric Guitar

The instrument, and the method for teaching it to yourself. Eight pages, no pentatonic yet — everything here applies whatever you end up practising.

Two things are being built at once:

What the instrument is. Sustain, compression, gain, string noise, and an amplifier that is part of the instrument rather than an accessory. Acoustic technique transfers about 60%; the missing 40% is mostly noise control.

A replacement for the teacher. A teacher is not primarily a source of information — information is free. A teacher is an error-detection system. Without one you will practice your mistakes with perfect discipline.
