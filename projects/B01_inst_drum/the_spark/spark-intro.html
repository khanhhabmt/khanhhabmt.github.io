⚡ The Spark

This is the "Front-end" of the project—the creative layer that keeps the motivation engine running. While "The Grind" focuses on optimization and technical debt, The Spark is about real-world implementation and exploration.

Sub-Modules​

  • Song Studies: Reverse-engineering the grooves of my favorite tracks.
  • Improvisation: Unstructured play to expand my rhythmic vocabulary.
  • Gear Bench: Hardware audits, kit configurations, and the inevitable "New Gear Day" logs.

Current Objective: Integrating Grade 2 dynamics into rock covers to see how they "feel" outside of an exam context.
