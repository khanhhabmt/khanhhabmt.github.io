Overview​

The 2025 version​

Alright, here's the story. For the last ten years, I've been living in the controlled chaos of data. My job is to jump into that mess of numbers and code to find the melody in the noise, turning massive datasets into something that actually speaks a human language. It's a riot, and it helps people make smarter moves (hopefully). I've led the projects, built the teams, and wrestled plenty of unruly data pipelines along the way.

The years are adding up, and I'm finding myself getting way more into the stuff that happens behind the scenes. A slick workflow, an infrastructure that's actually built to last. It’s not just about the data; it’s about the framework that handles it. And I've been lucky to share the studio with some killer people who’ve completely rewired how I think and live. You don't get anywhere without a solid team.

Through it all, the one thing I'm still totally into is that 'aha!' moment when an insight finally clicks. It's the best part.

Lately, my head is full of questions about what the next track is going to sound like. I'm wondering how GenAI is about to remix the entire world of analytics, and I'm here for it.

Co-written with a little help from my AI bandmate, Gemini.
